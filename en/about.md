# Kheireddine Riahi

I am a Solutions Architect with an interest in the design, transformation and governance of information systems.

This blog brings together practical guides across five areas:

- **Enterprise Architecture:** TOGAF, ADM, ArchiMate, capability mapping and governance.
- **Microservices Architecture:** DDD, APIs, events, Kafka, resilience and distributed systems.
- **Data Architecture:** Data Lakes, Lakehouses, Spark, streaming, governance and orchestration.
- **AI Architecture:** Machine Learning, Transformers, RAG, fine-tuning, LoRA, QLoRA and MLOps.
- **Architecture Patterns:** hexagonal architecture, CQRS, Saga, Event Sourcing, Circuit Breaker and BFF.

The goal is to connect concepts to practical examples, architecture decisions and reproducible demonstrations.
