Welcome to **kriahi.pro**, my space for sharing knowledge about information systems architecture, distributed architectures, Data and artificial intelligence.

The site is organized around five learning paths: Enterprise Architecture, Microservices Architecture, Data Architecture, AI Architecture and architecture patterns.

Each page presents core concepts, business examples, diagrams, points to watch and decision criteria for architects.

The ambition is simple: learn, experiment, document and share reusable approaches.
