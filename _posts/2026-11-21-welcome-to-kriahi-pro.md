This blog is a place to share ideas about designing and evolving information systems.

You will find practical content on:

- **IT Architecture:** TOGAF, ArchiMate, DDD, hexagonal architecture and microservices.
- **Data:** ingestion, streaming, Lakehouses, governance and analytics platforms.
- **Artificial Intelligence:** LLMs, RAG, agents, model serving and MLOps.
- **Cloud and DevOps:** Kubernetes, automation, observability and resilience.

The goal is to share applicable knowledge, architecture diagrams, demonstrations and lessons learned.
